## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Flughafen Wien, the Vienna airport group, grew revenue by 7.2% to EUR 1.1bn in FY2025, but EBITDA fell by 6.7% as write-offs on the abandoned third runway project and higher personnel costs weighed on the result.
2. The EBITDA margin narrowed by 5.5 points to 36.5%, and net leverage moved to net cash of EUR 414m from EUR 512m.
3. The point to watch is cash generation: free cash flow fell by 77.5% to EUR 61.7m as capex rose by 59.9% to EUR 272m, leaving cash liquidity to revenue at 41.6%.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA fell by 6.7% to EUR 412m on revenue up 7.2%, with the write-offs on the third runway project and environmental measures in the neighbouring municipalities among the special items.
2. Operating profit declined by 8.7% to EUR 280m as depreciation, amortisation and impairment eased by 2.1% to EUR 133m.
3. Total debt was stable at EUR 55.9m, so gross leverage held at 0.14x and the group kept net cash of EUR 414m, down by 19.1%.
4. Cash generation weakened: FFO fell by 31.0% to EUR 247m and cash from operations by 24.9% to EUR 333m, while capex intensity rose by 8.0 points to 24.1%.
5. Finance costs rose by 52.7% to EUR 4.3m, a small charge against an EBITDA of EUR 412m given the minimal debt stock.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 2.8% to 32.6m, with traffic growth of 2.8% against 7.3% in FY2024.
2. Aeronautical revenue per passenger increased by 3.1% to EUR 16.05, helped by charge adjustments under the price-cap formula and dampened by higher temporary winter incentives.
3. Personnel expenses rose on collective wage increases and additions to personnel provisions, partly related to underutilisation, and the loss of control over the Get2 subsidiary removed it from full consolidation.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments fell by 17.2% to EUR 470m, taking cash liquidity to revenue down by 12.3 points to 41.6%.
2. That cash pile covers the whole debt stock of EUR 55.9m many times over, so near-term maturities are met from existing cash without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments fell by 77.1% to EUR 62.9m, as net capex rose by 60.0% to EUR 270m.
