# Aena, FY2025

Fact sheet `b3d54f8fde26`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Aena, the Spanish airport operator, grew revenue by 8.9% to EUR 6.3bn in FY2025, and Moody's raised its long-term issuer and senior unsecured ratings during the year.
2. Net leverage fell by 0.11x to 1.46x, with EBITDA up 7.8% and net debt broadly unchanged at EUR 5.5bn.
3. The point to watch is cash generation: free cash flow fell by 10.2% to EUR 1.7bn as capex rose by 28.7% to EUR 1.1bn.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 7.8% to EUR 3.8bn, and operating profit rose by 12.2% to EUR 3.0bn as depreciation, amortisation and impairment fell by 6.0% to EUR 797m.
2. Total debt rose by 3.2% to EUR 7.6bn, but net debt was stable at EUR 5.5bn, so net leverage came down by 0.11x to 1.46x and gross leverage held at 2.00x.
3. Interest coverage improved by 2.38x to 14.72x, with finance costs broadly unchanged at EUR 242m.
4. FFO rose by 4.8% to EUR 3.0bn, lifting FFO to debt to 39.6%, but FCF to debt fell by 3.4 points to 22.9% as capex intensity rose by 2.6 points to 16.9%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Revenue rose by 8.9% to EUR 6.3bn, while cash from operations was broadly unchanged at EUR 2.8bn.
2. The EBITDA margin narrowed by 0.6 points to 60.3%, still far above the airport peer median of 36.5%.
3. In FY2024 the Spanish network carried 309.3m passengers, with traffic growth of 9.2% and aeronautical revenue per passenger of EUR 10.18.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments rose by 12.4% to EUR 2.0bn, and with EUR 2.2bn of undrawn committed facilities total liquidity stood at EUR 4.3bn, or 68.4% of revenue.
2. Debt due within one year fell by 35.5% to EUR 864m, so liquidity covers it 4.97x over and the near-term maturities are covered without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments, the comparable measure of cash left for debt holders, fell by 9.6% to EUR 1.8bn, or 23.2% of debt, as net capex rose by 28.0% to EUR 1.0bn.
4. On funding, the Brazilian subsidiary BOAB issued secured debentures maturing in the long term, London Luton airport completed the refinancing of its bank debt, and Fitch affirmed the group's ratings with a stable long-term outlook.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Aena has the highest EBITDA margin of the airport group (1st of 3).
2. It also leads the group on interest coverage (1st of 2), FFO to debt (1st of 2) and FCF to debt (1st of 2).
3. Leverage sits in the middle of the group, with net leverage ranking 2nd of 3 and gross leverage 2nd of 3, while capex intensity is below the peer median of 18.2%.

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash generation: free cash flow after leases fell by 9.6% to EUR 1.8bn and FCF after leases to debt by 3.3 points to 23.2%, as net capex rose by 28.0%.
2. Investment: capex intensity rose by 2.6 points to 16.9%, below the peer median of 18.2% but absorbing a larger share of revenue.
3. Margins: the EBITDA margin narrowed by 0.6 points to 60.3%, a level still well above the peer median of 36.5%.
4. Liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue rose by 1.0 point to 32.6%, in the middle of the group, and debt due within one year of EUR 864m is covered 4.97x over by liquidity.
