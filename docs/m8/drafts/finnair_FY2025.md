# Finnair, FY2025

Fact sheet `d5bf967be45a`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Finnair, the Finnish network carrier, closed FY2025 with revenue broadly unchanged at EUR 3.1bn and EBITDA of EUR 408m, as prolonged industrial action in the first half weighed on the result.
2. Net leverage fell by 0.44x to 1.77x as net debt was reduced by 26.4% to EUR 722m.
3. The point to watch is earnings quality: the EBITDA margin narrowed by 1.5 points to 13.1%, the weakest of the network peers (3rd of 3), with FFO to debt down to 18.0%.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA declined by 8.2% to EUR 408m on revenue of EUR 3.1bn, broadly unchanged on the prior year.
2. Operating profit fell by 43.8% to EUR 64.2m as depreciation, amortisation and impairment rose by 4.1% to EUR 344m.
3. Total debt was reduced by 4.4% to EUR 1.8bn, but gross leverage rose by 0.17x to 4.37x as EBITDA declined by 8.2%.
4. Interest coverage improved by 0.83x to 4.98x as finance costs fell by 23.6% to EUR 81.9m.
5. Cash generation weakened: FFO fell by 21.2% to EUR 320m and FCF by 49.1% to EUR 193m, taking FFO to debt down by 3.8 points to 18.0% and FCF to debt to 10.8%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers were broadly stable at 11.9m, a change of 2.0%, with the fleet at 79 aircraft and an average age of 14.3 years.
2. Fuel cost was broadly unchanged at EUR 879m, a change of 1.7%, and the fuel share of costs fell by 1.6 points to 28.9%.
3. Finnair purchased leased A321 aircraft during the year, and all aircraft were in operative use at the balance sheet date.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Liquidity rose by 16.3% to EUR 1.3bn, of which EUR 200m in undrawn committed facilities, broadly unchanged on the prior year, and cash and cash-like investments increased by 20.0% to EUR 1.1bn.
2. Near-term maturities are well covered: debt due within one year fell by 37.3% to EUR 182m, taking short-term debt cover up by 3.19x to 6.93x, and borrowings due within two years declined by 60.2% to EUR 72.5m, so liquidity covers them 17.39x times.
3. Cash flow after lease payments is the weaker measure: FCF after leases fell by 75.7% to EUR 50.8m, taking FCF after leases to debt down by 8.4 points to 2.8%, as cash from operations declined by 34.4% to EUR 402m.
4. Customer prepayments remain a material part of the funding base: contract liabilities rose by 8.5% to EUR 715m, while advance ticket cover fell by 7.1 points to 67.4%.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Finnair's strongest position is liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue is the highest of the network peers (1st of 3), against a median of 25.0%.
2. It is weakest on earnings and leverage: the EBITDA margin is the lowest of the group (3rd of 3), and gross leverage is above the peer median of 3.07x.
3. FCF to debt ranks 2nd of 3 and advance ticket cover 2nd of 3, so both sit in the middle of the group.

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash generation: FCF after leases fell by 75.7% to EUR 50.8m, only 2.8% of debt, so the cash left for debt holders after lease payments is thin.
2. Earnings: the EBITDA margin narrowed by 1.5 points to 13.1%, below the peer median of 15.3%, and FFO to debt fell by 3.8 points to 18.0% against a peer median of 27.4%.
3. Leverage: gross leverage rose by 0.17x to 4.37x, above the peer median of 3.07x, and interest coverage of 4.98x remains below the peer median of 8.06x.
4. Cost base: industrial action in the first half weighed on the result, and costs related to environmental regulation and navigation and landing charges added to it, with operating profit down 43.8% to EUR 64.2m.
