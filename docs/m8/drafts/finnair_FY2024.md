## Snapshot (draft-v7, needs_manual_editing)

1. Finnair, the Finnish network carrier, ended FY2024 with revenue up 2.0% to EUR 3.0bn but a lower earnings base, as industrial action in Finland led to the cancellation of a large number of flights in the early and late parts of the year.
2. The EBITDA margin narrowed by 3.4 points to 14.6%, and net leverage rose by 0.16x to 2.21x.
3. The point to watch is leverage against the network peers: net leverage of 2.21x is above the peer median of 1.88x and the weakest of the group (3rd of 3).

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA fell by 17.3% to EUR 445m, and operating profit declined by 40.3% to EUR 114m as depreciation, amortisation and impairment eased by 4.6% to EUR 330m.
2. Total debt was reduced by 7.9% to EUR 1.9bn and net debt by 11.1% to EUR 981m, yet gross leverage rose by 0.43x to 4.20x as EBITDA fell by 17.3%.
3. Interest coverage improved to 4.15x as finance costs fell by 24.6% to EUR 107m.
4. FFO declined by 7.4% to EUR 406m, leaving FFO to debt broadly unchanged at 21.8%, while free cash flow rose by 456.7% to EUR 379m as capex fell by 42.2% to EUR 234m.
5. FCF to debt rose by 16.9 points to 20.3%, and capex intensity fell by 5.8 points to 7.7%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 6.1% to 11.7m, while revenue grew by 2.0% to EUR 3.0bn.
2. Fuel cost was broadly unchanged at EUR 894m, with the fuel share of costs easing by 1.6 points to 30.5%.
3. The fleet was stable at 80 aircraft, with a new A350 delivered and leased A350 and A321 aircraft purchased during the year.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments fell by 4.1% to EUR 884m, and with EUR 200m of undrawn committed facilities liquidity stood at EUR 1.1bn, or 35.6% of revenue.
2. Debt due within one year rose by 2.0% to EUR 290m, covered 3.74x by liquidity, and borrowings due within two years of EUR 182m are covered 5.95x by liquidity, so near-term maturities are met without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments turned positive at EUR 209m, from EUR -130m in FY2023, lifting FCF after leases to debt by 17.6 points to 11.2%.
4. Customer prepayments rose, with contract liabilities up by 30.0% to EUR 659m and advance ticket cover at 74.5%, so a larger share of the cash balance is customers' money.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Finnair sits in the middle of the network group on EBITDA margin (2nd of 3), FCF to debt (2nd of 3) and cash liquidity to revenue (2nd of 3).
2. Leverage is the weakest part of the profile: net leverage and gross leverage are both the highest of the group (3rd of 3 and 3rd of 3).
3. Coverage and cash generation also trail the group, with interest coverage of 4.15x against a peer median of 6.51x and FFO to debt of 21.8% against 23.4%.
