# Wizz Air Holdings, FY2026 (Apr 2025 to Mar 2026)

Fact sheet `74a55194d2d7`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Wizz Air, the Central and Eastern European low-cost carrier, grew revenue by 8.0% to EUR 5.7bn in FY2026 while the Pratt & Whitney GTF engine issue continued to ground aircraft and constrain capacity, utilisation and cost performance.
2. The EBITDA margin widened by 1.7 points to 23.2%, and net leverage fell by 0.49x to 3.79x.
3. The point to watch is leverage against peers: net leverage of 3.79x is above the low-cost median of 1.19x, the weakest of the group (3rd of 3).

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 16.2% to EUR 1.3bn, but operating profit fell by 16.6% to EUR 140m as depreciation, amortisation and impairment rose by 21.9% to EUR 1.2bn.
2. Total debt rose by 5.6% to EUR 7.0bn and net debt by 3.1% to EUR 5.0bn, yet gross leverage fell by 0.53x to 5.28x on the larger earnings base.
3. FFO rose by 20.9% to EUR 1.1bn, lifting FFO to debt by 2.0 points to 15.2%, while interest coverage improved by 0.31x to 5.39x despite finance costs rising by 7.3% to EUR 268m.
4. Free cash flow was broadly unchanged at EUR 772m as cash from operations rose by 10.7% to EUR 1.2bn and capex rose by 44.4% to EUR 408m, so FCF to debt slipped by 0.8 points to 11.1%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 10.0% to 69.7m on capacity up 8.5% to 132.0bn available seat kilometres, with revenue passenger kilometres up 7.9% to 119.9bn.
2. The load factor slipped by 0.5 points to 90.8%, in line with the peer median of 90.8%.
3. Unit revenue was stable, with RASK at 4.31 euro cents and passenger yield at 2.64 euro cents, while CASK was broadly unchanged at 4.20 euro cents as CASK ex-fuel rose by 5.7% to 2.87 euro cents.
4. Fuel cost was stable at EUR 1.8bn and its share of costs fell by 3.4 points to 31.8%, above the peer median of 28.2%.
5. The fleet grew by 13.4% to 262 aircraft with an average age of 4.6 years, and the company renegotiated its remaining Airbus order book, deferring deliveries.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments rose by 12.4% to EUR 2.0bn, equal to cash liquidity to revenue of 34.3%, up 1.3 points on the prior year.
2. Debt due within one year fell by 46.2% to EUR 816m, so cash on the balance sheet covers the near-term maturities without new funding, and the group repaid its eurobond out of free cash during the year.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments rose by 276.4% to EUR 538m, lifting FCF after leases to debt by 5.5 points to 7.7%, the measure that leaves lease payments in financing cash flows.
4. Customer prepayments remain a large part of the funding base: contract liabilities rose by 16.5% to EUR 1.2bn, with advance ticket cover at 60.5%.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Wizz Air has the highest EBITDA margin of the low-cost group (1st of 3), at 23.2% against a median of 21.2%.
2. Leverage is the weakest part of the profile: net leverage ranks last of the group (3rd of 3), against a median of 1.19x, and gross leverage also ranks last (3rd of 3) against a median of 2.46x.
3. Cash generation trails the group, with FFO to debt and FCF to debt below medians of 35.4% and 14.6%, and interest coverage below a median of 6.27x.

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Leverage: net leverage of 3.79x is above the peer median of 1.19x and ranks last of the group (3rd of 3), so the balance sheet remains the weakest part of the profile.
2. Cash generation: FCF to debt slipped by 0.8 points to 11.1%, below the peer median of 14.6%, as capex intensity rose by 1.8 points to 7.2%.
3. Customer prepayments: advance ticket cover rose by 2.1 points to 60.5%, above the peer median of 55.1% and the weakest of the group (3rd of 3), so a larger share of liquidity is refundable if bookings slow.
4. Costs: CASK ex-fuel rose by 5.7% to 2.87 euro cents while the GTF engine issue continued to ground aircraft and weigh on utilisation and cost performance.
