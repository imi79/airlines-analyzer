# Groupe ADP, FY2024

Fact sheet `d07484f3361b`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Groupe ADP, the Paris airport operator, grew revenue by 12.1% to EUR 6.2bn in FY2024, while the EBITDA margin narrowed by 2.0 points to 33.7%.
2. Net leverage was broadly unchanged at 4.13x, as net debt rose by 8.2% to EUR 8.6bn and EBITDA grew by 6.0%.
3. The point to watch is cash generation: free cash flow fell by 50.3% to EUR 355m as capex rose by 34.1%, and cash liquidity to revenue declined by 10.8 points to 34.0%.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 6.0% to EUR 2.1bn on revenue up 12.1% to EUR 6.2bn, but operating profit fell by 20.0% to EUR 994m as depreciation, amortisation and impairment were stable at EUR 791m and finance costs declined by 46.6% to EUR 595m.
2. Total debt rose by 2.7% to EUR 10.7bn and net debt by 8.2% to EUR 8.6bn, so gross leverage eased to 5.14x while net leverage was broadly unchanged at 4.13x.
3. Interest coverage improved to 6.97x as finance costs declined by 46.6%, and FFO was stable at EUR 1.5bn, leaving FFO to debt broadly unchanged at 14.0%.
4. Cash from operations declined by 4.0% to EUR 1.5bn while capex rose by 34.1% to EUR 1.2bn, cutting free cash flow by 50.3% to EUR 355m and FCF to debt to 3.3%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. The Paris airports handled 103.4m passengers in FY2024.
2. In October 2024 the group acquired Paris Experience Group and P/S, extending the Extime offering beyond the Paris airports.
3. Capex intensity rose by 3.1 points to 19.0%, reflecting the investment programme across the airport network.
4. DIAL filed its tariff proposal for the next regulatory period in May 2024, with final tariffs published in March 2025 and effective from April 2025.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments fell by 14.8% to EUR 2.1bn, taking cash liquidity to revenue down to 34.0%.
2. Debt due within one year declined by 4.3% to EUR 1.8bn, which cash and cash-like investments of EUR 2.1bn cover, so the near-term maturities do not depend on new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments fell by 89.8% to EUR 42.0m, only 0.4% of total debt, as net capex rose by 33.6% to EUR 1.2bn.
