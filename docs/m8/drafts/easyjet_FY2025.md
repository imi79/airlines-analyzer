# easyJet, FY2025 (Oct 2024 to Sep 2025)

Fact sheet `47d1edb73d34`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. easyJet, the UK low-cost carrier, grew revenue by 8.6% to GBP 10.1bn in FY2025 while holding its EBITDA margin broadly unchanged at 14.2%.
2. The balance sheet strengthened: net leverage fell by 0.29x to -0.42x, a net cash position, as total debt was reduced by 10.8%.
3. The point to watch is liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue fell by 2.3 points to 34.9%, and the EBITDA margin of 14.2% is the weakest of the low-cost peers (3rd of 3).

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA rose by 5.9% to GBP 1.4bn on revenue up 8.6%, and operating profit increased by 18.2% to GBP 696m as depreciation fell by 3.5%.
2. Total debt was cut by 10.8% to GBP 2.9bn, taking gross leverage down by 0.38x to 2.03x and leaving net cash of GBP 602m against GBP 181m in FY2024.
3. Cash generation improved: FFO rose by 3.0% to GBP 1.3bn, lifting FFO to debt by 5.9 points to 44.0%, and free cash flow rose by 16.4% to GBP 624m despite capex up 7.8% to GBP 1.0bn.
4. Interest coverage eased by 1.08x to 9.22x as finance costs rose by 18.2% to GBP 156m, with airline net financing charges up on lower interest income on cash and higher charges on borrowings.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Capacity grew by 9.4% to 134.5bn available seat kilometres and traffic by 9.3% to 122.0bn revenue passenger kilometres, with passengers up 4.1% to 93.4m.
2. CASK fell by 1.4% to 7.00 pence while CASK ex-fuel was stable at 5.32 pence, and fuel cost was broadly unchanged at GBP 2.3bn, taking the fuel share of costs down by 1.6 points to 23.9%.
3. The fleet rose by 2.6% to 356 aircraft, with an average age of 10.9 years after the purchase of aircraft previously held on lease.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments were broadly unchanged at GBP 3.5bn, and with GBP 1.3bn of undrawn committed facilities liquidity reached GBP 4.8bn, or 47.4% of revenue.
2. Debt due within one year fell by 60.0% to GBP 257m, covered 18.65x by liquidity, so near-term maturities are met without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments fell by 8.0% to GBP 402m, equal to 13.7% of debt, as net capex rose by 23.7% to GBP 997m.
4. Customer prepayments rose, with contract liabilities up 12.0% to GBP 1.9bn and advance ticket cover up 4.9 points to 55.1%, so a larger share of the cash belongs to customers; the group also entered into a new revolving credit facility that replaced its previous facilities.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. easyJet has the lowest net leverage of the low-cost group (1st of 3), at -0.42x against a peer median of 1.19x, and also leads on gross leverage (1st of 3), interest coverage (1st of 3), FFO to debt (1st of 3) and FCF to debt (1st of 3).
2. The weakest point is profitability: the EBITDA margin of 14.2% is the lowest of the group (3rd of 3) against a peer median of 21.2%.
3. Capex intensity sits in the middle of the group at 9.9%, advance ticket cover is in the middle of the group at 55.1%, and cash liquidity to revenue is the highest of the group (1st of 3).

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue fell by 2.3 points to 34.9%, and free cash flow after leases declined by 8.0% to GBP 402m as net capex rose by 23.7%.
2. Profitability: the EBITDA margin of 14.2% is the lowest of the low-cost group (3rd of 3), against a peer median of 21.2%.
3. Customer prepayments: advance ticket cover rose by 4.9 points to 55.1%, above the level of a year earlier, which raises the sensitivity of the cash position to bookings.
4. Interest: coverage eased by 1.08x to 9.22x as finance costs rose by 18.2% to GBP 156m, with airline net financing charges up on lower interest income on cash and higher charges on borrowings.
