## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Air France-KLM, the network airline group spanning Air France and KLM, grew revenue by 4.9% to EUR 33.0bn in FY2025, with the EBITDA margin widening by 2.2 points to 15.3%.
2. Net leverage fell by 0.17x to 1.71x, in line with the peer median, even as net debt rose by 12.3% to EUR 8.7bn.
3. The point to watch is cash liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue fell by 1.2 points to 20.8%, below the peer median, while capex intensity rose by 1.6 points to 13.5%.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 23.0% to EUR 5.1bn, and operating profit rose by 36.6% to EUR 2.0bn as depreciation, amortisation and impairment grew by 15.6% to EUR 3.1bn.
2. Total debt rose by 5.9% to EUR 15.5bn, but gross leverage fell by 0.49x to 3.07x on the larger earnings base.
3. Interest coverage improved by 1.55x to 8.06x, with finance costs broadly unchanged at EUR 627m.
4. FFO rose by 24.1% to EUR 4.3bn, lifting FFO to debt by 4.0 points to 27.4%, and free cash flow turned positive at EUR 606m as cash from operations grew by 44.6% to EUR 5.1bn.
5. FCF to debt improved by 5.5 points to 3.9%, held back by capex of EUR 4.4bn, up 19.3%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Revenue grew by 4.9% to EUR 33.0bn, with the EBITDA margin at 15.3% against 13.1% in FY2024.
2. Capex intensity rose by 1.6 points to 13.5%, above the peer median of 10.4%, as the group invested in its fleet.
3. In FY2024 the group carried 98.0m passengers with a fleet of 564 aircraft at an average age of 12.1 years.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments were stable at EUR 6.9bn, a change of 1.1%, and cash liquidity to revenue fell by 1.2 points to 20.8%, below the peer median of 25.0%.
2. Debt due within one year rose by 3.3% to EUR 2.8bn, so the near-term maturities are covered by cash on hand without new funding.
3. In FY2024 free cash flow after lease payments was EUR -1.1bn, or -7.6% of debt, showing that lease payments absorbed the cash that free cash flow left out.
4. Customer prepayments rose, with contract liabilities up 4.1% to EUR 4.3bn, and advance ticket cover increased by 3.1 points to 62.2%, the lowest of the peer group (1st of 3).
