# Air France-KLM, FY2025

Fact sheet `3b553c628d82`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Air France-KLM, the network airline group spanning Air France and KLM, grew revenue by 4.9% to EUR 33.0bn in FY2025, with the EBITDA margin widening by 2.2 points to 15.3%.
2. Net leverage fell by 0.17x to 1.71x, in line with the peer median, even as net debt rose by 12.3% to EUR 8.7bn.
3. The point to watch is cash liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue fell by 1.2 points to 20.8%, below the peer median, while capex intensity rose by 1.6 points to 13.5%.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 23.0% to EUR 5.1bn, and operating profit rose by 36.6% to EUR 2.0bn as depreciation, amortisation and impairment grew by 15.6% to EUR 3.1bn.
2. Total debt rose by 5.9% to EUR 15.5bn, but gross leverage fell by 0.49x to 3.07x on the larger earnings base.
3. Interest coverage improved by 1.55x to 8.06x, with finance costs broadly unchanged at EUR 627m.
4. FFO rose by 24.1% to EUR 4.3bn, lifting FFO to debt by 4.0 points to 27.4%, and free cash flow turned positive at EUR 606m as cash from operations grew by 44.6% to EUR 5.1bn.
5. FCF to debt improved by 5.5 points to 3.9%, held back by capex of EUR 4.4bn, up 19.3%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Revenue grew by 4.9% to EUR 33.0bn, with the EBITDA margin at 15.3% against 13.1% in FY2024.
2. Capex intensity rose by 1.6 points to 13.5%, above the peer median of 10.4%, as the group invested in its fleet.
3. In FY2024 the group carried 98.0m passengers with a fleet of 564 aircraft at an average age of 12.1 years.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments were stable at EUR 6.9bn, a change of 1.1%, giving cash liquidity to revenue of 20.8%.
2. Debt due within one year rose by 3.3% to EUR 2.8bn, while borrowings due within two years fell by 21.5% to EUR 3.1bn, so cash on hand covers the near-term maturities without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments turned positive at EUR 367m, from EUR -1.1bn in FY2024, taking FCF after leases to debt up by 10.0 points to 2.4%.
4. Customer prepayments rose, with contract liabilities up by 4.1% to EUR 4.3bn, and advance ticket cover rose by 3.1 points to 62.2%, so a larger part of the cash balance is customers' prepayments.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Air France-KLM sits in the middle of the network group on EBITDA margin, net leverage, gross leverage, interest coverage and FFO to debt, each ranking 2nd of 3.
2. Its strongest position is advance ticket cover, the lowest of the group (1st of 3), against a peer median of 67.4%.
3. It is weakest on cash generation and liquidity, with FCF to debt against a peer median of 10.8% (3rd of 3) and cash liquidity to revenue the lowest of the group (3rd of 3).

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue fell by 1.2 points to 20.8%, below the peer median of 25.0%.
2. Cash generation: FCF to debt of 3.9% is below the peer median of 10.8%, the weakest of the group (3rd of 3), with capex intensity at 13.5% above the peer median of 10.4%.
3. Maturities: debt due within one year rose by 3.3% to EUR 2.8bn, and cash flow after leases covers only 2.4% of debt, so refinancing depends on access to the bond market, where the group placed senior unsecured bonds under its EMTN programme.
4. Customer prepayments: advance ticket cover rose by 3.1 points to 62.2%, so a larger part of the cash balance is customers' prepayments.
