## Snapshot (draft-v7, needs_manual_editing)

1. Wizz Air, the low-cost carrier operating an all-Airbus narrowbody fleet, saw its EBITDA margin narrow by 2.0 points to 21.5% in FY2025, a year in which a manufacturer engine recall forced a large part of the fleet to be grounded at any time.
2. Net leverage rose by 0.38x to 4.28x as EBITDA fell by 4.9% to EUR 1.1bn.
3. The point to watch is leverage against the peer group: net leverage of 4.28x compares with a peer median of 0.78x, and Fitch downgraded the company citing higher leverage and increased costs.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. Revenue rose by 3.8% to EUR 5.3bn, but EBITDA fell by 4.9% to EUR 1.1bn as CASK rose by 10.1% to 4.19 euro cents.
2. Operating profit fell by 61.7% to EUR 168m as depreciation, amortisation and impairment rose by 28.0% to EUR 967m.
3. Total debt increased by 5.5% to EUR 6.6bn and net debt by 4.2% to EUR 4.9bn, taking gross leverage up by 0.58x to 5.81x.
4. Interest coverage fell by 1.93x to 5.08x as finance costs rose by 26.8% to EUR 250m.
5. FFO fell by 13.3% to EUR 872m, so FFO to debt declined by 2.9 points to 13.2%, while free cash flow rose by 139.9% to EUR 783m as cash from operations increased by 60.4% and capex fell by 16.4%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 2.2% to 63.4m while capacity was broadly unchanged, with ASK broadly stable at 121.7bn available seat kilometres.
2. The load factor improved by 1.0 point to 91.3%, with RPK broadly stable at 111.1bn.
3. RASK rose by 3.9% to 4.33 euro cents, but CASK ex-fuel rose by 18.9% to 2.71 euro cents, so unit cost outpaced unit revenue.
4. Fuel cost fell by 3.1% to EUR 1.8bn, and the fuel share of costs declined by 4.8 points to 35.2%.
5. The fleet grew by 11.1% to 231 aircraft, with an average age of 4.5 years, as new A321neo aircraft were delivered and older A320ceo aircraft redelivered.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments rose by 9.3% to EUR 1.7bn, taking cash liquidity to revenue up by 1.7 points to 33.0%.
2. Debt due within one year rose by 40.0% to EUR 1.5bn, so cash liquidity covers the near-term maturities only in part and the balance depends on refinancing.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments rose by 17.0% to EUR 143m, but at 2.2% of debt it was broadly unchanged and leaves little cash for debt holders once leases are paid.
4. Customer prepayments rose: contract liabilities increased by 27.1% to EUR 1.0bn, and advance ticket cover rose by 8.2 points to 58.4%, so a larger share of liquidity is cash received for future travel.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Wizz Air has the highest EBITDA margin of the low-cost peer group (1st of 3), at 21.5% against a median of 18.9%.
2. Leverage is the weakest part of the comparison: net leverage of 4.28x ranks 3rd of 3 against a peer median of 0.78x, and FFO to debt ranks 3rd of 3 against a median of 35.6%.
3. Cash liquidity to revenue and capex intensity both sit in the middle of the group, with cash liquidity to revenue ranking 2nd of 3.

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Leverage: net leverage rose by 0.38x to 4.28x, the weakest of the low-cost peer group (3rd of 3) and well above the peer median of 0.78x.
2. Cash generation: FFO to debt fell by 2.9 points to 13.2%, below the peer median of 35.6%, as FFO declined by 13.3%.
3. Maturities: debt due within one year rose by 40.0% to EUR 1.5bn against cash and cash-like investments of EUR 1.7bn, so refinancing risk is concentrated in the near term.
4. Customer prepayments: advance ticket cover of 58.4% is the highest of the peer group (3rd of 3), so liquidity relies on cash received for future travel.
