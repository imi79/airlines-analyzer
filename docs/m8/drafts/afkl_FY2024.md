# Air France-KLM, FY2024

Fact sheet `644e36880f91`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Air France-KLM, the Franco-Dutch network airline, grew revenue by 4.8% to EUR 31.5bn in FY2024 while EBITDA was broadly unchanged at EUR 4.1bn.
2. Net leverage rose by 0.59x to 1.88x as net debt increased by 43.5% to EUR 7.7bn, and the EBITDA margin narrowed by 0.8 points to 13.1%.
3. The point to watch is cash generation: free cash flow turned negative at EUR -232m with capex up 5.0% to EUR 3.7bn, and cash liquidity to revenue fell by 5.5 points to 22.0%.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA was broadly unchanged at EUR 4.1bn on revenue up 4.8% to EUR 31.5bn, but operating profit fell by 12.6% to EUR 1.5bn as depreciation, amortisation and impairment rose by 5.9% to EUR 2.6bn.
2. Total debt increased by 7.4% to EUR 14.6bn, so gross leverage rose by 0.29x to 3.56x and net leverage by 0.59x to 1.88x.
3. Interest coverage eased by 0.45x to 6.51x as finance costs rose by 5.2% to EUR 631m.
4. FFO rose by 2.1% to EUR 3.4bn, but FFO to debt fell by 1.2 points to 23.4% as total debt increased by 7.4%, and FCF to debt dropped by 2.0 points to -1.6%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 4.7% to 98.0m, and the fleet grew by 4.3% to 564 aircraft at an average age of 12.1 years.
2. Fleet renewal was delayed by supply chain constraints and engine problems on part of the new-generation fleet, which prevented the group from operating those aircraft at maximum capacity.
3. The operating result included a payment to Virgin in a contract renegotiation and a net provision for the restructuring of the domestic offer from Orly and of call centres.
4. Capex intensity was broadly unchanged at 11.9%, with capex of EUR 3.7bn against cash from operations of EUR 3.5bn.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments fell by 16.0% to EUR 6.9bn, taking cash liquidity to revenue down to 22.0%.
2. Debt due within one year rose by 6.4% to EUR 2.7bn, and borrowings due within two years stood at EUR 3.9bn, so the near-term maturities are covered by cash liquidity without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments was EUR -1.1bn, down from EUR -615m in FY2023, and FCF after leases to debt fell by 3.1 points to -7.6%, so cash left for debt holders was negative after leases.
4. Customer prepayments rose: contract liabilities increased by 6.2% to EUR 4.1bn, with advance ticket cover up by 12.4 points to 59.1%.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Air France-KLM has the weakest EBITDA margin of the network airlines (3rd of 3), at 13.1% against a peer median of 14.6%.
2. Leverage, interest coverage and FFO to debt all sit in the middle of the group, with net leverage ranking 2nd of 3 and gross leverage 2nd of 3.
3. Cash liquidity to revenue is the weakest of the group (3rd of 3), while advance ticket cover is the lowest (1st of 3).

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash generation: free cash flow after leases was EUR -1.1bn and FCF after leases to debt fell by 3.1 points to -7.6%, so the group consumed cash after lease payments.
2. Liquidity: cash liquidity to revenue fell by 5.5 points to 22.0%, below the peer median of 29.0%.
3. Maturities: debt due within one year rose by 6.4% to EUR 2.7bn, against cash and cash-like investments of EUR 6.9bn that fell by 16.0%.
4. Margins: the EBITDA margin narrowed by 0.8 points to 13.1%, the weakest of the network airlines (3rd of 3).
