## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Groupe ADP, the Paris airport group that also consolidates TAV Airports, grew revenue by 8.9% to EUR 6.7bn in FY2025, with the Ankara Esenboga concession renewal entering service during the year.
2. The EBITDA margin widened by 1.0 point to 34.7%, and net leverage fell by 0.45x to 3.68x.
3. The point to watch is cash liquidity, which fell by 3.9 points to 30.1% of revenue, the weakest of the airport peers (3rd of 3).

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

2. Interest coverage improved by 0.92x to 7.89x even as finance costs rose by 11.4% to EUR 663m.
3. FFO rose by 11.2% to EUR 1.7bn, lifting FFO to debt by 1.7 points to 15.7%, while free cash flow increased by 18.3% to EUR 420m as capex intensity eased by 0.8 points to 18.2%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers reached 107.0m, up 3.5%, with traffic growth of 3.5%.
2. Revenue rose by 8.9% to EUR 6.7bn, faster than passenger volumes, helped by the international portfolio.
3. Depreciation, amortisation and impairment charges rose, reflecting an unfavourable base effect after the prior year's impairment reversal on the Amman concession.
4. The Ankara Esenboga concession renewal entered service in May 2025, adding operating rights in exchange for capacity investment.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments fell by 3.8% to EUR 2.0bn, taking cash liquidity to revenue down by 3.9 points to 30.1%.
2. Debt due within one year rose by 4.1% to EUR 1.9bn, which cash and cash-like investments of EUR 2.0bn cover, so the near-term maturities do not depend on new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments was EUR 42.0m in FY2024, or 0.4% of debt, a small fraction of the EUR 355m free cash flow reported before lease payments.
4. The TAV group's guarantee of part of the bank loan financing the initial Antalya concession payment no longer exists at year-end after the refinancing of the previous bank loan.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

2. Leverage is the weakest in the group, with net leverage of 3.68x against a peer median of 1.46x (3rd of 3).
3. Capex intensity sits in the middle of the group, while FFO to debt of 15.7% and FCF to debt of 4.0% are below the peer medians of 27.7% and 13.4%.
