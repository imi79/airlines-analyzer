# easyJet, FY2024 (Oct 2023 to Sep 2024)

Fact sheet `0a7b7f959b04`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. easyJet, the UK low-cost carrier, grew revenue by 13.9% to GBP 9.3bn in FY2024, with EBITDA up 20.7% to GBP 1.4bn.
2. The EBITDA margin widened by 0.8 points to 14.6%, and the group held net cash of GBP 181m, so net leverage stayed at -0.13x.
3. The point to watch is cash generation: free cash flow fell by 32.7% to GBP 536m as capex rose by 23.2% to GBP 929m.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA rose by 20.7% to GBP 1.4bn, and operating profit by 30.0% to GBP 589m, as depreciation, amortisation and impairment increased by 14.4% to GBP 770m.
2. Total debt increased by 13.7% to GBP 3.3bn, but gross leverage fell by 0.15x to 2.41x and the group ended the year with net cash of GBP 181m.
3. Interest coverage improved by 4.04x to 10.30x as finance costs fell by 26.7% to GBP 132m.
4. FFO rose by 31.3% to GBP 1.3bn, lifting FFO to debt by 5.1 points to 38.1%.
5. Cash generation weakened: cash from operations fell by 5.5% to GBP 1.5bn while capex rose by 23.2% to GBP 929m, so FCF to debt dropped by 11.3 points to 16.3%.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 8.3% to 89.7m on capacity up 8.4% to 122.9bn available seat kilometres, with revenue passenger kilometres up 8.4% to 111.6bn.
2. The load factor was stable at 90.8% and passenger yield broadly unchanged at 5.12 pence.
3. RASK rose by 5.1% to 7.58 pence, but CASK rose by 4.2% to 7.10 pence and CASK ex-fuel by 5.4% to 5.29 pence, so the unit cost base moved against the group.
4. Fuel cost rose by 9.3% to GBP 2.2bn, while fuel's share of costs fell by 0.8 points to 25.5%.
5. The fleet grew by 3.3% to 347 aircraft, with an average age of 10.2 years, as NEO aircraft joined the fleet and a large number remained on order.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments rose by 18.3% to GBP 3.5bn, taking cash liquidity to revenue up by 1.4 points to 37.2%, above the peer median of 33.0%.
2. Debt due within one year was stable at GBP 643m, so cash on the balance sheet covers the near-term maturities without new funding, and the group issued a euro-denominated guaranteed bond under its EMTN programme maturing in 2031.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments fell by 33.3% to GBP 437m, and FCF after leases to debt dropped by 9.4 points to 13.3%, the comparable measure of cash left for debt holders once lease payments in financing are taken out.
4. Customer prepayments remain a large part of the funding base: contract liabilities rose by 16.0% to GBP 1.7bn, while advance ticket cover eased by 1.0 point to 50.2%, in the middle of the peer group.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. easyJet has the lowest net leverage of the low-cost group (1st of 3), and the strongest interest coverage (1st of 3) and FFO to debt (1st of 3).
2. The EBITDA margin of 14.6% is the weakest of the group (3rd of 3), below the peer median of 18.9%.
3. Gross leverage, FCF to debt and advance ticket cover all sit in the middle of the group, and cash liquidity to revenue is the highest of the peers (1st of 3).

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash generation: FCF after leases to debt fell by 9.4 points to 13.3%, so less cash is left for debt holders after lease payments and capital spending.
2. Investment: capex intensity rose by 0.8 points to 10.0%, above the peer median of 5.4%, with net capex up 18.9% to GBP 806m.
3. Unit costs: CASK ex-fuel rose by 5.4% to 5.29 pence, with significant investment in spares made during the year to limit the time aircraft are grounded by supply-chain delays.
4. Profitability: the EBITDA margin of 14.6% is the weakest of the low-cost group (3rd of 3), below the peer median of 18.9%.
