# Flughafen Wien, FY2024

Fact sheet `0e3e42e11637`. Sections marked **draft-v8** were redrafted in M8; the others keep their draft-v7 text (M4).

## Snapshot (draft-v7, passed)

1. Flughafen Wien, the Vienna airport group, closed FY2024 with revenue up 13.0% to EUR 1.1bn and EBITDA up 12.3% to EUR 442m, while the complete repayment of interest-bearing financial liabilities left the balance sheet in net cash.
2. Net leverage fell by 0.24x to -1.16x, a net cash position, with the EBITDA margin broadly unchanged at 42.0%.
3. The point to watch is capital spending: capex intensity rose by 5.6 points to 16.1% and free cash flow fell by 4.6% to EUR 274m.

## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 12.3% to EUR 442m on revenue up 13.0% to EUR 1.1bn, and operating profit rose by 16.9% to EUR 306m as depreciation rose by 3.1% to EUR 136m.
2. Total debt was stable at EUR 55.6m, so gross leverage held at 0.13x and net cash rose by 41.4% to EUR 512m.
3. Cash generation improved: FFO rose by 3.4% to EUR 358m and cash from operations by 15.3% to EUR 444m, while finance costs fell by 76.3% to EUR 2.8m.
4. Free cash flow declined by 4.6% to EUR 274m as capex rose by 73.6% to EUR 170m.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 7.3% to 31.7m, although traffic growth of 7.3% was below the prior year's 24.7%.
2. Aeronautical revenue per passenger increased by 7.6% to EUR 15.57, supported by the temporary inflation-linked adjustment of airport charges.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments rose by 36.0% to EUR 567m, taking cash liquidity to revenue up by 9.1 points to 53.9%.
2. With the interest-bearing financial liabilities fully repaid, the cash pile covers the group's borrowings outright, so near-term maturities do not depend on new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments fell by 4.5% to EUR 275m, as net capex rose by 74.0% to EUR 169m.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Flughafen Wien has the lowest net leverage of the airport group (1st of 3), at -1.16x against a peer median of 1.57x, and the lowest gross leverage (1st of 3).
2. Cash liquidity to revenue is the strongest of the group (1st of 3), against a peer median of 34.0%.
3. The EBITDA margin sits in the middle of the group at 42.0%, as does capex intensity at 16.1%, while traffic growth is the weakest of the group (2nd of 2).

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Investment: capex intensity rose by 5.6 points to 16.1%, in the middle of the peer group, and net capex rose by 74.0% to EUR 169m.
2. Cash flow after leases: free cash flow after lease payments fell by 4.5% to EUR 275m, so the cash left for debt holders narrowed even with net cash on the balance sheet.
3. Traffic: traffic growth of 7.3% was below the peer median of 8.3% and the weakest of the group (2nd of 2).
4. Regulation: airport charges are temporarily adjusted by average inflation, a rule that ends at the end of 2026, so aeronautical revenue per passenger of EUR 15.57 depends on the tariff framework that follows.
