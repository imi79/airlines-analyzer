## Credit metrics (draft-v7, passed)

1. EBITDA increased by 16.1% to EUR 3.5bn, and operating profit rose by 20.9% to EUR 2.7bn as depreciation, amortisation and impairment grew by 3.2% to EUR 848m.
2. Total debt was reduced by 14.8% to EUR 7.3bn and net debt by 11.6% to EUR 5.5bn, so gross leverage fell by 0.76x to 2.08x.
3. Cash generation strengthened: FFO rose by 20.3% to EUR 2.9bn, taking FFO to debt up by 11.3 points to 39.0%, and free cash flow rose by 129.9% to EUR 1.9bn as capex fell by 40.5% to EUR 823m.
4. Interest coverage eased by 0.27x to 12.34x as finance costs rose by 18.8% to EUR 246m.

## Operating performance (draft-v7, passed)

1. Passengers rose by 9.2% to 309.3m, although traffic growth slowed by 7.0 points to 9.2%.
2. Aeronautical revenue per passenger increased by 4.1% to EUR 10.18, while the CNMC set the maximum adjusted annual revenue per passenger for 2025 at the same level as the prior year.
3. Capex intensity fell by 13.1 points to 14.3%, and cash from operations rose by 23.7% to EUR 2.7bn.

## Liquidity and funding (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash and cash-like investments fell by 22.9% to EUR 1.8bn, which is the whole of the cash liquidity position and excludes undrawn credit lines.
2. Debt due within one year was reduced by 24.3% to EUR 1.3bn, so the near-term maturities are covered by cash liquidity without new funding.
3. Free cash flow after lease payments rose by 134.6% to EUR 1.9bn, equal to 26.5% of debt, the comparable measure of cash left for debt holders once leases are paid.
4. Net capex fell by 42.6% to EUR 794m, which released cash for debt reduction.

## Peer positioning (draft-v7, passed)

1. Aena has the highest EBITDA margin of the airport peers (1st of 3), at 60.9% against a median of 42.0%, and leads on interest coverage (1st of 2), FFO to debt (1st of 2) and FCF to debt (1st of 2).
2. Leverage sits in the middle of the group, with net leverage ranking 2nd of 3 and gross leverage 2nd of 3.
3. Cash liquidity to revenue is the weakest of the group (3rd of 3), against a peer median of 34.0%.

## Watch items (draft-v8, passed)

1. Cash liquidity: cash and cash-like investments fell by 22.9% to EUR 1.8bn, taking cash liquidity to revenue down by 15.3 points to 31.6%, below the peer median of 34.0%.
2. Maturities: debt due within one year of EUR 1.3bn is covered by cash liquidity, but the buffer is thinner than a year earlier as cash and cash-like investments fell by 22.9%.
3. Interest coverage: coverage eased by 0.27x to 12.34x as finance costs rose by 18.8% to EUR 246m, so the cost of debt is rising faster than EBITDA.
